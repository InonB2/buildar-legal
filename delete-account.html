Delete your BuildAR Pro account

Effective date: 2026-09-11

You can request permanent deletion of your BuildAR Pro account and all data associated with it.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your account and authentication record (email, password hash)
  • Your profile
  • All of your build sessions and progress (which projects you started, your current step, session status)
  • All of your activity events (session and step history, AI Assistant usage metadata)

Catalog content that isn't personal to you (for example the project/assembly guides themselves) is not affected. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

How to request deletion

Self-serve, in-app deletion is not yet available. To request deletion today, email:

support@buildarpro.com

from the email address associated with your BuildAR Pro account, with the subject line "Delete my BuildAR Pro account" (or similar). Please include the account email address in the body if it's different from the sending address.

Turnaround time

We process deletion requests within 30 days of receiving your email. You'll receive a confirmation once your account and data have been deleted.

Questions

See our Privacy Policy for more on what we collect and why, or contact support@buildarpro.com with any questions about this process.
