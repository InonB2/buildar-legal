1. Information we collect

1.1 Account information

When you create an account you provide an email address and a password. Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth. Your password is transmitted over HTTPS and stored by Supabase only as a salted hash; we never have access to your plaintext password. Supabase Auth also records standard authentication metadata (for example account creation time, last sign-in time, and the IP address used to authenticate) as part of providing the sign-in service. The App does not ask for your name, phone number, address, date of birth, or payment details.

1.2 Build activity

When you start building a project, the App creates a session on our server that stores: the project you selected, your current step index, the session status (active / completed / abandoned), and timestamps. As you move through the steps this record is updated. This data is tied to your account and is what lets you leave and later resume a build.

1.3 Activity events

Our server records activity events in our database, linked to your account, when you: start, resume, or complete a session; view or complete a step; or invoke the AI Assistant. For an Assistant event we also store the character count of your question (not the text of it), the response latency, the AI model name, and a content-safety verdict. We use these events for product analytics and to understand where users get stuck.

1.4 Camera frames during AR sessions

AR guidance is optional and requires camera permission. Only while an AR session is running, the App periodically captures a single still frame from the AR camera view, downscales it on your device to at most 800×800 pixels (JPEG), encodes it, and sends it over HTTPS with your authentication token to our API. Our API forwards the image to Google Cloud Vision API, which returns object- and text-detection results used to locate the part you are assembling and anchor the on-screen overlay.

These frames are not saved to your device's photo gallery, are not written to our database, and are not retained by our servers after the detection response is returned — they are processed transiently. Google processes the image to produce the detection result; Google's handling of Cloud Vision API requests is governed by the Google Cloud Platform terms and data processing terms.

The App does not record video or audio and has no access to your existing photos or media library.

1.5 AI Assistant

If you open the in-app AI Assistant and ask a question, the App sends over HTTPS to our API — and our API then sends to Anthropic's Claude API — the text of your question together with context about the project you are building (its title, summary, and the text of its steps). Anthropic returns the answer shown in the App. We do not store the text of your question or the answer; we store only the event metadata described in section 1.3. Anthropic's processing of API traffic is governed by Anthropic's commercial terms and data-usage policies.

1.6 Local storage on your device

The App stores the following on your device only, using the operating system's app storage (AsyncStorage): your Supabase authentication session (so you stay signed in across restarts) and a single "active session" record used for the offline resume banner (session ID, project ID, project name, current step index, total step count, and a timestamp). Your saved authentication session is cleared when you sign out. The resume record is cleared when you finish that build, and otherwise expires automatically after 7 days; uninstalling the App removes all local data.

1.7 Operational logs

Our API is hosted on Railway and writes rotating operational log files. These may include your IP address, request timestamps, the API path called, and your account's user identifier (a UUID). They do not contain your password, your camera frames, or the body of your AI Assistant questions. Logs are rotated on a short schedule and used for security, abuse prevention, and debugging.

2. How we use your information

We use the data above to: create and secure your account; authenticate you and let you reset your password; provide the core build-guidance features (projects, steps, sessions, resume); provide AR part detection and overlay placement; answer your questions through the AI Assistant; measure product usage and improve the App; and detect, prevent, and investigate abuse, security incidents, and technical problems.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not share it with data brokers.

4. International data transfers

Our sub-processors may process and store data on servers located outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union. Where data is transferred internationally, it is protected by the contractual and security terms of the relevant provider. All data in transit between the App, our production backend, and our sub-processors is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS (see the Security section).

5. Data retention

  • Account data (email, hashed password, auth metadata, profile record): kept until you delete your account.
  • Build sessions and activity events: kept until you delete your account, after which they are removed automatically (deletion of your account cascades to your profile, your sessions, and your activity events).
  • AR camera frames: not retained — processed transiently and discarded after the detection response.
  • AI Assistant question text: not retained by us; handled by Anthropic according to its data-usage and retention policies for API traffic.
  • Local device data: the saved authentication session is cleared on sign-out; the resume record is cleared when you finish that build and otherwise self-expires after 7 days; uninstalling the App removes all local data.
  • Operational logs: retained only briefly and rotated on a short schedule.

6. Deleting your account and data

You can request deletion of your account and its associated data in any of these ways:

  1. In the App — open the account/settings screen and choose "Delete account". (In-app self-serve deletion is not yet live; use one of the methods below until it ships.)
  2. On the web — visit our account-deletion page and follow the instructions. This page is publicly accessible and does not require the App.
  3. By email — write to support@buildarpro.com from the address associated with your account.

When your account is deleted, we delete your authentication record, your profile, your build sessions, and your activity events. Catalog content that is not personal to you (for example project definitions) is unaffected. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Camera frames and AI Assistant question text are not stored, so there is nothing further to delete on our side for those.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can exercise access, correction, and deletion rights using the contact details below or the deletion methods in section 6. Under Israel's Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, you may request to review and correct information we hold about you.

8. Security

All connections from the App to our production backend and from our backend to our sub-processors use HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit. (The App also supports a plain-HTTP endpoint on the local loopback address for on-device development builds only; it is never used against production servers or real user data.) Access to your account data is protected by authentication and database row-level security so that your sessions and events are only accessible to you. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes by Supabase Auth. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

9. Children

BuildAR Pro is a do-it-yourself assembly tool intended for a general adult audience. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 16 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction and by our authentication provider's terms). If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your continued use of the App after an update takes effect means you accept the revised policy.
